# Company Overview

Brightleaf Design Co. is a four-person branding and packaging studio founded in 2022.
We work with small food, fitness, and retail brands who need a full identity: logo,
packaging, and a launch-ready style guide, usually within six weeks.

Founders: [[Alex Rivera]] (creative director) and [[Jamie Chen]] (operations and client
strategy). We currently run three active engagements: [[Acme Corp]], [[Lumen Bakery]],
and [[Porter Fitness]].

Every engagement follows the [[Brand Discovery Process]] and starts with the
[[Onboarding Checklist]]. Pricing follows the [[Pricing Guide]].
