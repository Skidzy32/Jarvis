# Instagram Strategy

Brightleaf's own marketing, separate from client work. Goal: one process-behind-the-
scenes post and one finished-work post per week.

Best performing content so far has been early concept sketches from the [[Brand
Discovery Process]], not final polished logos — followers engage more with the
messy middle than the clean result.

Finished [[Lumen Packaging]] proofs are a strong candidate for next week's post once
Maren signs off on public sharing. Always get written client sign-off before posting
any client work; template lives in the [[Case Study Template]].
