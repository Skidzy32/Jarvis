# Brand Discovery Process

Our standard five-phase approach, used on [[Acme Rebrand]], [[Lumen Packaging]], and
[[Porter App UI]] alike, though timelines flex with scope:

1. Discovery + audit — competitor review, stakeholder interviews, mood boards
2. Concept round 1 — two to three directions, presented live, never emailed cold
3. Concept round 2 — one direction refined based on feedback
4. Refinement — production-ready files, style guide draft
5. Handoff — final assets, style guide, and a 30-minute "how to use this" call

[[Alex Rivera]] insists on live presentations for round 1 specifically because
async feedback on early concepts tends to be shallow and design-by-committee.
