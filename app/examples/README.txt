EXAMPLE NOTES (not yours)
=========================

These are the made-up "Brightleaf Design Co." notes that came with the
original Jarvis pack: a small branding studio with clients, projects,
team members and processes. They were moved here from notes/ in 2.3.0 so
Jarvis's brain stops answering from them and your own record starts
fresh. Nothing was deleted.

Jarvis ignores this folder. To look at them in the galaxy again for a
while, you could copy them back into notes/ -- or run
    python build.py examples
to build the galaxy from these instead (the next start of Jarvis
rebuilds it from your real notes/ folder again).
