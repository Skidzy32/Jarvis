# Onboarding Checklist

Used for every new client before kickoff, most recently for [[Porter Fitness]].

1. Signed contract and 50% deposit received
2. Kickoff call scheduled within 5 business days of signing
3. Shared drive folder created and access sent
4. Intake questionnaire sent (brand history, competitors, must-avoid colors/fonts)
5. Add client to the [[2026 Q3 Invoices]] tracker with milestone dates
6. First [[Brand Discovery Process]] session booked
