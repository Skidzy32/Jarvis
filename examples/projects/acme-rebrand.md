# Acme Rebrand

Full identity project for [[Acme Corp]]. Deliverables: primary logo, secondary
wordmark, signage system for two new stores, and packaging for the private-label tool
line (14 SKUs).

Timeline follows the [[Brand Discovery Process]]:
- Discovery + audit: complete
- Concept round 1: complete, two directions presented
- Concept round 2: in progress, client chose the "Forged" direction
- Refinement: current phase
- Handoff + style guide: targeted for 2026-11-01

Risk: signage vendor lead time is six weeks, so final logo files need to lock by
2026-10-01 or the spring store opening slips. Flagged to [[Jamie Chen]].
