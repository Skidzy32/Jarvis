# Pricing Guide

Standard rates, maintained by [[Jamie Chen]].

- Full rebrand (logo + full style guide + packaging or signage): $35,000–$50,000,
  billed in three milestones (40% / 40% / 20%)
- Packaging-only engagement: $8,000–$15,000 flat fee
- App/product UI engagement: $15,000–$25,000, billed in two milestones (50% / 50%)
- Change orders / out-of-scope work: $175/hour, billed monthly

All new engagements start with a 50% deposit collected during the [[Onboarding
Checklist]] before any design work begins.
