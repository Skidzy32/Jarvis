# Lumen Bakery

A single-location artisan bakery expanding into wholesale grocery distribution. Owner
is Maren Ostlund. She found us through a referral from a past client.

Scope: packaging redesign for their wholesale line (six SKUs) plus a simplified logo
mark that works small on bread bags. Project details in [[Lumen Packaging]].

This is a smaller engagement, $11,500 flat fee, no milestone structure. Kickoff was
2026-08-01. [[Jamie Chen]] is running point since the scope is narrow enough not to
need [[Alex Rivera]] on every call.

Maren is decisive and fast to approve concepts, which has kept this project ahead of
schedule relative to the [[Brand Discovery Process]] timeline.
