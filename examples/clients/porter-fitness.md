redesign to go with a broader rebrand. Contact is co-owner Devon Porter.

discussed but shelved for budget reasons; revisit in Q1 2027.

Budget: $18,000. Kickoff 2026-08-20. [[Alex Rivera]] is designing, [[Jamie Chen]] is
managing scope creep, since Devon has already asked for two features outside the
original brief.
